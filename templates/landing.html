LIVE OPHTHALMIC PIPELINE
Classical Image Processing (IVP) + MobileNetV2 Deep Learning
STAGE 01

Green Channel

Extracts peak hemoglobin absorption band for maximum vascular and lesion contrast.

STAGE 02

FOV Circle Mask

Segments circular retinal aperture to eliminate peripheral black background artifacts.

STAGE 03

Adaptive CLAHE

Tile-based local contrast equalization enhances subtle microaneurysms and drusen deposits.

STAGE 04

Top-Hat Morphology

White Top-Hat structural opening isolates vessel arborization and computes Vessel Density %.

STAGE 05

Optic Disc & CDR

Measures Cup-to-Disc Ratio via localized ellipse fitting and inner pale cup saturation.

STAGE 06

Lesion Segmentation

Multi-spectral absorption ratio flags hemorrhages (red) and drusen deposits (yellow).

Dual-Engine Decision Fusion Core

P = 0.35 CNN + 0.65 IVP

Mathematical IVP biomarkers actively govern and cross-validate MobileNetV2 deep neural representations to guarantee clinical accuracy without black-box hallucination.

MULTI-LABEL CLASSIFICATION

5-Class Multi-Disease Screening

Simultaneously detect Normal, Diabetic Retinopathy, Glaucoma, Age-Related Macular Degeneration, and Cataract from a single fundus photograph with calibrated softmax probabilities.

IMAGE PROCESSING

CLAHE & Morphological Top-Hat

Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization combined with White Top-Hat filtering reveals microvascular details invisible to the naked eye, improving detection of subtle hemorrhages and drusen.

DEEP LEARNING BACKBONE

MobileNetV2 Neural Network

MobileNetV2 deep residual backbone with depthwise separable convolutions and inverted bottlenecks, fine-tuned on multi-class retinal pathology datasets for high-efficiency clinical inference.

HIGH-THROUGHPUT RUNTIME

Sub-200ms CPU Inference

Complete preprocessing and neural inference in under 200 milliseconds — fast enough for real-time clinical screening workflows on standard CPU-only hardware without requiring discrete GPUs.
